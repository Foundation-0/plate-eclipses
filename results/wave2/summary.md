## Sensitivity limits (what the null excludes — VERIFIED computation)

catalogued eclipse is in principle detectable per plate. The binding limit
is plate sampling: the blind search needs >=3 faint nights with no normal
plate between. Caught fractions below are the share of 1890–1990 start

Reading (CONJECTURE): the nulls are informative mainly against long
(>=100 d), deep (>= catalogue depth) dimmings, and even then exclude only
the stated fraction of the century per target. They say little about
short or shallow eclipses, and nothing about whether these VSX `EA:`
classifications (several flagged type-uncertain, KNOWN caveat from
TARGETS-V2) are real. Sub-threshold >=1-night faint clusters exist on most
targets at counts consistent with the comparison-star false-alarm level of
the original null test; none is promoted.

## Limitations and NOT done

- NSV 2587 and NSV 1565 fields yield only 2 comparison stars under the
  `fetch.py` rule. Their target correction therefore rests on 2 stars, and
  the per-comparison null cannot run (a 1-star zero-point never satisfies
  `core.plates`' >=2-residual rule, so 0 comparisons were testable). Their
  nulls carry that caveat; widening the comparison rule would deviate from
  the template and was not done.
- No plate-image cutout re-measurement: there were no candidates to
  re-measure (the control events are already published/re-measured).
- No period scan on Tier-A: periods and modern epochs are unknown, so
  there is nothing to anchor `period.py` on; none was invented.
- No literature check on these 8 VSX entries beyond TARGETS-V2's flags.
- DASCH light curves are cached in git-ignored `data/lc/` (~130 curves
  fetched this run, target curves largely reused from TARGETS-V2).

## Failed attempts / bugs found in this run

- First version of `tier_a_targets()` re-sorted Tier-A by raw score, which
  promoted PRIOR-only rows with no DASCH match (NSV 11236 etc.) over
  proxy-scored rows. Caught when three intended targets did not run;
  fixed to CSV rank order before they were searched. The five targets run
  before the fix are all genuinely in the top 8, so no result changed.
- No DASCH fetch failures. No target was skipped.
